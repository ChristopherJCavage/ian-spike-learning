# Steps -- Chameleon Colour-Match

Human connection language. Numbers **must** match `0 STEP` in `model.ldr`
(LeoCAD step 1 = first `0 STEP`). Template: `context/connection-language.md`.

Rewritten for the build-tester pass: the hub is flipped 180 degrees about
Y from earlier drafts, so the wall with the two round cable ports is now
the *rear* wall (over the tail) and the short wall with the little USB
shape is the *front* wall (over the brow). The hub stays Bright Yellow --
a draft of this pass briefly called it White off a table photo taken for
the eye-peg point below, but LEGO's own catalogue confirms this element
has no white variant, so that is reverted. The tongue is gone -- Ian's
table found a flex hose cannot mount on the sensor -- replaced by two
black pegs and two white round eye tiles.

Step 1 -- hub
  Sub: body
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Yellow Small Hub | kit 45345
  Do: Stand the Small Hub on the table. The wall with the two round
      cable ports faces away from you; the short wall with the small
      USB shape faces you.
  Check: The hub sits flat and does not rock. Looking at the wall
      facing you, you see the little USB shape, not the two round
      ports.

Step 2 -- hip pins
  Sub: legs
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 4x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45345
  Do: Look at the holes on the hub's long side walls -- there are
      three rows, one above the other. Push a peg into each of the
      four holes on the BOTTOM row only (two per side, the row
      closest to the table). Leave the other two rows empty.
  Check: Four pegs stick out low on the hub, two on the left and two
      on the right, all at the same height, just above the table.

Step 3 -- hips
  Sub: legs
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Reddish Violet 1x4 Technic brick | kit 45345
  Do: Press one 1x4 brick onto each pair of pegs, studs pointing down
      toward the table, so the brick hangs under the hub like a hip.
  Check: Both hips sit flush on the hub sides. The studs face the
      table.

Step 4 -- sockets
  Sub: legs
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 4x Medium Stone Grey ball-cup plate | kit 45345
  Do: Press a cup plate onto the two downward studs at each end of
      each hip, cups facing out to the sides.
  Check: Four cups stick out, two per side, front and back. They do
      not wiggle off.

Step 5 -- feet
  Sub: legs
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 4x Dark Stone Grey ball-plate | kit 45345
  Do: Pop a ball-plate into each cup, then splay the front feet a
      little forward and the back feet a little backward so they do
      not bump.
  Check: The chameleon stands on four ball-joint feet. You can pose a
      leg and it stays.

Step 6 -- front pins
  Sub: head
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45345
  Do: Push a peg into each of the two holes on the wall facing you --
      the short wall with the little USB shape, not the wall with the
      round ports.
  Check: Two pegs stick straight forward, level with each other.

Step 7 -- brow bricks
  Sub: head
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Orange 1x2 Technic brick | kit 45345
  Do: Press an orange 1x2 brick onto each front peg, studs up --
      these are two separate small bricks, not one long one, so look
      for a thin gap between them.
  Check: Two small orange bricks, one on each peg, with a thin gap
      between them.
  Note: Each brick is held by ONE peg, so each one can still twist.
      That is expected here -- do not try to line them up perfectly
      yet. The next piece is what holds them both straight.

Step 8 -- brow plate
  Sub: head
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Green 2x8 plate with holes | kit 45345
  Do: Press the green plate down across BOTH orange bricks at once,
      so it bridges the gap between them.
  Check: Two separate orange bricks sit side by side under the green
      plate, which lies flat across both of them. Nothing wiggles.

Step 9 -- cheek bumps
  Sub: head
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Reddish Violet round slide shoe 2x2 | kit 45345
  Do: Reach up underneath the middle of the green plate -- between
      the two bricks, where nothing is in the way yet -- and press a
      round shoe into each of the two open tubes there.
  Check: Two round purple bumps peek out from under the middle of the
      plate, one on each side of the centre line. Not eyes -- there is
      no housing here for an eye to look out of, just two cheeks.

Step 10 -- brow horns
  Sub: head
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 4x Bright Reddish Violet 1x1 cone | kit 45345
  Do: Stand two cones on each side of the brow, both on the very last
      stud column at the outside edge, one behind the other.
  Check: A row of two purple horns down each outside edge of the
      brow.

Step 11 -- T-beams
  Sub: head
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Red T-beam with 1x2 plate | kit 45345
  Do: Press a T-beam onto the front row of studs on the brow, one on
      the left and one on the right, so the pin-holes in the T face
      forward like nostrils.
  Check: Two red T's stand on the brow, holes looking at you.

Step 12 -- snout pins
  Sub: head
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45345
  Do: Push a peg into each T-beam hole, so the pegs point forward.
  Check: Two pegs stick out of the T's, same height, ready for the
      sensor.

Step 13 -- colour sensor
  Sub: head
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x White Colour Sensor | kit 45345
  Do: Press the Colour Sensor onto those two pegs so the lens points
      down at the table. Its cable does not plug in yet -- both ports
      are on the back wall, much later.
  Check: The lens looks at the floor, not at you. Peek under the
      belly -- you should see the dark window.

Step 14 -- eye pins
  Sub: face
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45345
  Do: Push a peg into each of the two holes on the front face of the
      sensor, pointing straight out toward you. Push each one about
      halfway in, so half of it stands proud.
  Check: Two short pegs stick out of the sensor's face, side by side,
      the same length.

Step 15 -- eyes
  Sub: face
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x White round tile 1x1 | kit 45345
  Do: Press a white round tile onto the tip of each eye peg -- push
      straight on until it stops.
  Check: Two white eyes look out from the front of the sensor. A
      gentle twist is fine; they stay on.

Step 16 -- top pins
  Sub: shoulders
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45345
  Do: Stand a peg in each of the two pin-holes on top of the hub, up
      near the brow end.
  Check: Two pegs stand up on top of the hub, near the head.

Step 17 -- deck
  Sub: shoulders
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Blue 7-module beam | kit 45345
  Do: Lower the 7M beam onto those two posts so it lies across the
      hub over the shoulders, holes facing up.
  Check: The beam sits level, just behind the brow. It does not
      see-saw.

Step 18 -- matrix pins
  Sub: shoulders
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45345
  Do: Stand a peg in each of the two holes near the middle of the
      deck, pointing up.
  Check: Two pegs stand up from the blue beam, closer together than
      the top pins.

Step 19 -- light matrix
  Sub: shoulders
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Medium Azur 3x3 Colour Light Matrix | kit 45345
  Do: Press the light matrix onto those pegs so the nine lights face
      up. Its cable does not plug in yet either.
  Check: You can see all nine lights from above, sitting over the
      shoulders, just behind the brow.

Step 20 -- rear pins
  Sub: tail
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45345
  Do: Push a peg into each of the two holes on the wall with the two
      round cable ports -- one to each side of the ports.
  Check: Two pegs stick straight back, level with each other, framing
      the two ports.

Step 21 -- tail bar
  Sub: tail
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Dark Azur 1x8 Technic brick | kit 45345
  Do: Push the long blue brick onto both rear pegs at once, studs up.
  Check: The blue bar runs the full width of the hub. It does not
      teeter.

Step 22 -- tail plate
  Sub: tail
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Green 2x8 plate with holes | kit 45345
       2x Bright Orange round tile 2x2 with hole | kit 45345
  Do: While the green plate is still loose in your hand, press a
      round orange tile onto each end of it. Then press the whole
      plate down onto the blue bar.
  Check: The green plate sits flat on the bar with an orange circle
      near each end. It does not teeter.

Step 23 -- tail corners
  Sub: tail
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Orange flat tile 1x2 | kit 45345
  Do: Cap the plate's two far corners -- the ones away from the hub --
      with the flat tiles, laid front to back.
  Check: Four orange patches on the tail shelf: two round circles
      near the hub, two flat rectangles at the far corners.

Step 24 -- tail base
  Sub: tail
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Dark Green 2x4 brick with cross hole | kit 45345
  Do: Turn the dark green 2x4 brick so its long side points straight
      back, away from the head, and press it onto the four middle
      studs of the tail shelf.
  Check: The brick sticks out behind the chameleon and does not rock.
      This is the thickest part of the tail -- everything after it
      gets thinner.

Step 25 -- the curl
  Sub: tail
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 3x Flame Yellowish Orange 2x4 plate with holes | kit 45345
       1x Bright Reddish Violet 2x2 roof tile | kit 45345
  Do: Turn a light-orange plate sideways -- across the animal, not
      along it -- and press it onto the last four studs of the dark
      green brick so most of it swings out to one side. Press the
      next plate onto the last four studs of that one, and the
      third onto the last four of the second, each one a little
      higher. Then put the purple slope on the far end, ramp
      pointing out the same way.
  Check: The tail turns a corner and steps up three orange plates,
      ending in a purple point past the feet. Dark green, then
      orange, then orange, then orange, then purple.

Step 26 -- plug in and try it
  Sub: body
  Bin: Essential
  Add: (none -- use the colour pads Chris brought)
  Do: Plug the sensor's cable into port A and the light matrix's
      cable into port B, both on the hub's back wall. Pose the legs.
      Slide a coloured pad under the belly, in front of the lens.
  Check: Both cables reach the back wall and plug in easily. The feet
      stay where you put them. The pad fits under the belly without
      knocking the sensor.

Step 27 -- code
  Sub: code
  Bin: Essential
  Add: (none)
  Do: In Icon Blocks, make four separate stacks -- one yellow "when
      colour" hat per pad (green, blue, yellow, red), each lighting the
      matrix on port B the same colour. Do not snap the hats into one row.
  Check: Blue pad -> blue back. Yellow pad -> yellow back. Green pad
      -> green back. Red pad -> bright warning-red back.
