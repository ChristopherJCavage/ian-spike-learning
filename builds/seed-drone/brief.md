# Brief -- Seed Drone

Status: **filled**.

## Theme / story

Careers and technologies used to study nature. A child is the field
operator for a low-flying reforestation drone: survey a LEGO landscape,
find the bare red clearing where a tree was cut, and release a seed there.

The machine connects three ideas:

- ecologists map where a habitat has been damaged;
- remote-sensing tools help people find the right place to work;
- restoration crews can carry seeds to places that are hard to reach.

The model is handheld rather than airborne. Calling that out is part of
the lesson: this is a working model of sensing and payload release, not a
claim that LEGO motors can lift a SPIKE Hub.

## Complexity

- Target page count: 27 build pages + 1 mission page + 1 Icon Blocks page.
- Chris helps with the Hub-facing steps, the motor axle, the downward

  shoes, build the handle, and assemble the rotor.
- Sub-assemblies: `skids`, `seed bay`, `survey sensor`, `handle`, `rotor`,
  then `mission` and `code`.
- One action per page. The largest page adds four identical parts.

## Hardware

- Hub: **Essential Small Hub**, one only.
- Motor: **one Small Angular Motor** on port B, driving the tipping tray.
- Sensor: **Colour Sensor** on port A, lens aimed down.
- Rotor: hand-flicked, free-spinning, no motor.
- Payload: one loose Bright Red **BALL D16.5** (`54821`).
- Done-when: skim the sensor over the red drop zone; the tray tips,
  releases one seed, plays a short cue from the tablet, and returns level.

## Constraints

- Deliberately Essential-only: every model part is listed in
  `kits/45345-essential/parts.txt`.
- Both Hub ports are used. The second motor and 3x3 Light Matrix stay in
  the box.
- Kid screens use Icon Blocks. Essential streams the program, so the
  tablet must remain connected by Bluetooth during the handheld mission.
- The sensor only reads a target reliably at short range. The landing
  shoes put its lens about 41 LDU (16.4 mm) above the paper.
- The seed is payload, not structure. Gravity holds it in a 60-LDU-clear
  well; no fake clutch is invented for it.
- The rotor must clear the child's hand on the tail grip. Flick gently;
  never motorise it or pretend the model flies.
- Four 3M axles, both 11M beams, and all five 2x8 plates with holes are
  consumed by this build. They are not spare substitutions.
