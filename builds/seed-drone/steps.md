# Steps -- Seed Drone

Human connection language. Numbers **must** match `0 STEP` in `model.ldr`
(LeoCAD step 1 = first `0 STEP`).

The little USB-shaped wall is the **nose**. The wall with the two round
cable ports is the **back**. Keep those words fixed throughout the build.

The seed bay hangs off the back on the drone's centreline, and the two
short purple bars on the sides are the **grips** -- one for each hand.

Step 1 -- hub
  Sub: body
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Yellow Small Hub | kit 45345
  Do: Stand the yellow Hub on the table. Turn the little USB shape toward
      you and the two round cable ports away from you.
  Check: The Hub sits flat. You see the USB shape, not the round ports.

Step 2 -- landing pins
  Sub: skids
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 4x Black connector peg with friction | kit 45345
  Do: Turn the Hub upside down without changing which end is the nose.
      On each long side, push pegs into the two bottom holes nearest the
      nose and back corners. Use four matching corner holes; do not use
      the extra middle hole that appears on only one side.
  Check: Four pegs point down from the Hub, two on each side, making a
      rectangle. The left and right pairs match.

Step 3 -- landing plates
  Sub: skids
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Green 2x8 plate with holes | kit 45345
  Do: Turn each long green plate front-to-back. Slide one onto each pair
      of downward pegs. Leave the small gap made by the exposed peg.
  Check: Two green rails run beside the Hub from nose to back. They are
      level and do not touch the yellow shell.

Step 4 -- skid shoes
  Sub: skids
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Reddish Violet round slide shoe 2x2 | kit 45345
  Do: Press one purple round shoe upward into the middle of each green
      landing plate.
  Check: Turn the drone upright. It stands on two purple shoes; the Hub,
      green plates, and nose do not scrape the table.

Step 5 -- tail pins
  Sub: tail
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg with friction | kit 45345
  Do: At the back wall with the two round cable ports, put one peg in the
      hole on each side of the ports.
  Check: Two pegs point backward and frame the ports without covering
      either opening.

Step 6 -- tail spine
  Sub: tail
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Dark Azur 1x8 Technic brick | kit 45345
  Do: Press the long blue brick across both rear pegs, studs up. This
      brick is the backbone the whole seed bay hangs from, and it also
      shields the two cable ports.
  Check: The brick spans the full back of the Hub and does not teeter.
      You can still reach both round ports underneath it.

Step 7 -- spine pins
  Sub: tail
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg with friction | kit 45345
  Do: The blue brick has seven holes. Both end holes are already full --
      the step 5 pegs run right through the brick into the Hub. Find the
      middle hole. Put one peg in each of the two holes beside it, both on
      the same side, matching the picture. They point backward.
  Check: Two new pegs point straight back from two holes that touch each
      other. On one side of the pair is a full end hole; on the other side
      is the empty middle hole.
  Note: A mirror image works just as well. If your two pegs ended up on
      the other side of the middle hole, keep the rest of the tail on that
      side too and the drone is still correct.

Step 8 -- tail bracket
  Sub: tail
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Green 3x5 right-angle beam | kit 45345
  Do: Hold the green L-shaped beam on edge, like a wall, with its long
      arm lying flat along the blue brick and its short arm pointing
      **up**. Press the long arm onto both new pegs.
  Check: The long arm is horizontal and the short arm climbs. Its holes
      look straight back at you, not up at the ceiling.

Step 9 -- bracket pins
  Sub: tail
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg with friction | kit 45345
  Do: Put one peg in the bottom hole of the climbing arm and one in the
      top hole. Skip the middle hole. Both point backward.
  Check: Two pegs stick straight back, one above the other, with one
      empty hole between them.

Step 10 -- seed-bay motor
  Sub: seed bay
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x White Small Angular Motor | kit 45345
  Do: Stand the motor upright and press its flat back onto both pegs at
      once, so the turquoise round output points straight back, away from
      the nose. Let the cable hang toward the ports.
  Check: The motor sits flush against the green bracket and does not
      swivel. The turquoise output is centred behind the drone.

Step 11 -- tray axle
  Sub: seed bay
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Red 6M cross axle | kit 45345
  Do: Push the red axle straight back into the turquoise motor output.
      Leave the long end pointing away from the drone.
  Check: The axle points straight back down the middle of the drone and
      turns only when the motor turns.

Step 12 -- tray hinge
  Sub: seed bay
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Yellow 1x2 Technic brick with cross hole | kit 45345
  Do: Slide the yellow brick onto the far end of the red axle. Keep its
      two studs facing up. This brick does not need turning -- its cross
      hole already runs the right way.
  Check: The axle passes through the cross hole. Both yellow studs are
      level and face the ceiling.

Step 13 -- first tray plate
  Sub: seed bay
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Flame Yellowish Orange 2x4 plate with holes | kit 45345
  Do: Turn the orange plate front-to-back, so its long side runs away from
      you. Press it down onto both yellow hinge studs. The plate sits
      centred over the hinge, two studs wide.
  Check: The plate lies flat and front-to-back, and the yellow hinge brick
      is hidden underneath the middle of it.

Step 14 -- tray walls
  Sub: seed bay
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Reddish Violet 1x4 Technic brick | kit 45345
  Do: Press one purple wall along the far edge of the plate and one along
      the near edge. Each wall is four studs long and the plate is only
      two studs wide, so two studs sit on the plate and two hang off the
      right-hand side.
  Check: Two purple walls face each other. Two studs of each wall hang
      into empty space -- that is what the next plate clicks onto.

Step 15 -- make it square
  Sub: seed bay
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Flame Yellowish Orange 2x4 plate with holes | kit 45345
  Do: Turn the second orange plate the same way and press it under the
      hanging studs, against the first plate. Two plates side by side
      make one square floor, four studs each way.
  Check: The floor is a square, not a long strip. The hanging wall studs
      now sit on the new plate, so nothing flaps.
  Note: Plates next to each other do not grip. The walls have to go on
      first so the second plate has something to click onto.

Step 16 -- end stop
  Sub: seed bay
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Orange 1x2 Technic brick | kit 45345
  Do: Turn the little orange brick front-to-back and drop it into the left
      end of the channel, right beside the hinge. It is exactly two studs
      long and the channel is exactly two studs wide, so it fills the gap
      with nothing over and nothing short.
  Check: The tray now has three sides: two long purple walls and this
      short orange one. The right-hand end -- the one farthest from the
      drone's middle -- is wide open.

Step 17 -- seed
  Sub: seed bay
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Red D16.5 ball | kit 45345
  Do: Push the red ball down into the channel, near the closed orange end.
      It is a hair wider than the gap, so it will not drop to the floor --
      it wedges between the tops of the two purple walls and stays there.
      Do not force it; a firm press is enough.
  Check: The ball sits proud of the walls and does not touch the floor.
      Shake the drone gently: the seed stays put. Now tip the tray over by
      hand and the seed rolls out the open end.
  Note: A ball loose in a wide tray rattles about and can leave before you
      want it to. Gripped between two walls it only leaves when the tray
      turns over, which is exactly what the motor does.

Step 18 -- grip pins
  Sub: grips
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 4x Black connector peg with friction | kit 45345
  Do: Face the USB nose. On each side wall of the Hub, find the middle
      row of holes and use its front and back holes -- skip the one in
      between. Two pegs per side.
  Check: Four pegs stick straight out sideways, two on the left and two
      on the right, all at the same height.

Step 19 -- hand grips
  Sub: grips
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Reddish Violet 1x4 Technic brick | kit 45345
  Do: Turn each purple brick front-to-back and press it onto one pair of
      side pegs.
  Check: One short purple bar sticks out from each side, level with the
      other. Now pick the drone up: cup a hand around each side of the
      yellow Hub and rest each thumb on top of a purple bar. The drone
      comes up flat and level, and both hands stay well below the top of
      the Hub.
  Note: The bars are thumb rests, not carry handles -- they stick out only
      8 mm. The Hub body is what you actually hold. The bars tell your
      hands where to go, and they keep both thumbs low and outside the
      circle the blades will sweep.

Step 20 -- nose pins
  Sub: survey sensor
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg with friction | kit 45345
  Do: Push one peg into each hole on the short front wall with the little
      USB shape.
  Check: Two pegs point straight forward at the same height.

Step 21 -- nose bricks
  Sub: survey sensor
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Orange 1x2 Technic brick | kit 45345
  Do: Push one orange brick onto each nose peg, studs pointing up.
  Check: Two orange bricks, one on each peg, with a gap between them.
  Note: Each brick is held by ONE peg, so each one can still swivel. That is
        expected on this page -- do not try to line them up perfectly yet.
        The next piece is what locks them both straight.

Step 22 -- the face
  Sub: survey sensor
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Green 2x8 plate with holes | kit 45345
  Do: Press the long green plate down across BOTH orange bricks at once, so
      it bridges the gap between them.
  Check: Two separate orange bricks sitting under one flat green shelf.
      Neither brick can swivel now. This shelf is going to be the drone's
      face -- the sensor and the eyes go on it.

Step 23 -- sensor brackets
  Sub: survey sensor
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Red T-beam with 1x2 plate | kit 45345
  Do: Press both red T-beams onto the front row of the green shelf, one
      on each side of the centre. Point both round holes forward. Leave
      the two outside corners of the shelf empty -- the eyes go there.
  Check: The two red holes look at you like a pair of eyebrows, and there
      is a clear 2x2 patch of green studs beyond each one.

Step 24 -- sensor pins
  Sub: survey sensor
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg with friction | kit 45345
  Do: Push one peg through each red T-beam hole so both point forward.
  Check: Two black pegs stand parallel, ready for the sensor.

Step 25 -- colour sensor
  Sub: survey sensor
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x White Colour Sensor | kit 45345
  Do: Press the Colour Sensor onto both pegs with its dark lens pointing
      straight down at the table. Leave the cable unplugged for now.
  Check: Peek under the nose: you can see the lens. It does not point
      forward, sideways, or up.

Step 26 -- eye pins
  Sub: face
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg with friction | kit 45345
  Do: Look at the front of the dark sensor -- the face pointing away from
      the Hub. There are two holes side by side. Push one peg into each
      one so both stick straight out forward.
  Check: Two short black pegs point out of the front of the sensor, level
      with each other, like two little stalks. They do not touch the red
      brackets behind them.
  Note: These go in the FRONT holes. The two pegs that hold the sensor on
      are already in the back holes, from step 24.

Step 27 -- eyes
  Sub: face
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x White 1x1 round tile with printed eye | kit 45345
  Do: Find the two small round white tiles with an eye printed on them --
      a black ring with a dot in the middle. Press one onto the tip of
      each black peg.
  Check: The sensor now has two eyes and the drone is looking at you.
      Wiggle one gently: it grips the peg and stays on.
  Note: A peg is almost exactly as thick as a stud, so the little tube
      under the tile grips it the same way it grips a stud. This is the
      same trick the chameleon uses for its eyes.

      The picture on the instruction page shows a blank white circle,
      because the drawing program cannot print. The real tile has the eye.

      There are four of these tiles in the box. The chameleon uses two and
      the drone uses the other two, so both can have eyes -- but there is
      no spare pair. If one goes missing the drone builds fine without it.

Step 28 -- rotor-deck pins
  Sub: rotor
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg with friction | kit 45345
  Do: Stand two pegs in the pair of top holes nearest the nose.
  Check: Both pegs point straight up from the yellow Hub.

Step 29 -- rotor bearing
  Sub: rotor
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Blue 7M beam | kit 45345
  Do: Lower the blue beam across both top pegs. Keep its row of round
      holes facing up.
  Check: The beam lies left-to-right across the Hub. Its centre round
      hole is directly above the middle of the drone.

Step 30 -- free-spin axle
  Sub: rotor
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Black 4M cross axle | kit 45345
  Do: Stand the black axle through the centre ROUND hole of the blue
      beam. Do not use a cross-shaped hole here.
  Check: The axle can turn inside the round beam hole when you twist it.
  Note: This is the whole trick of a spinning rotor. A cross axle slides
      and turns freely in a round hole, and locks solid in a cross hole.
      The round hole here is the bearing; the cross hole in the next step
      is the lock.

Step 31 -- lock the hub
  Sub: rotor
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Dark Green 2x4 brick with cross holes | kit 45345
  Do: Slide the middle cross hole of the dark-green brick down onto the
      black axle, until the brick sits on the blue beam below.
  Check: Turn the brick. The black axle turns with it, and the blue beam
      underneath stays still. That is the point of a cross hole: it grips
      the axle instead of letting it spin.

Step 32 -- the rotor plate
  Sub: rotor
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Green 2x8 plate with holes | kit 45345
  Do: Press the long green plate down across all eight studs of the
      dark-green brick, centred so the same amount hangs off each end.
  Check: Seven round holes in a row along the top, with the same number
      of holes past the brick on the left as on the right. The plate and
      brick turn together with the axle.

Step 33 -- blade fasteners
  Sub: rotor
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Yellow 3M cross axle | kit 45345
       2x Black connector peg with friction | kit 45345
  Do: Find the middle hole of the green plate -- the black axle is already
      in it. Push one YELLOW axle into the hole on each side of it, right
      next to the black axle, and push each one all the way down until it
      stops. It should feel like it grabs.
      Then push one BLACK PEG into the very last hole at each end of the
      plate, as far out as the holes go.
  Check: Four posts standing up: two yellow either side of the middle, one
      black at each end. Tug a yellow axle upward -- it should not come
      out. If it slides out easily, it is not in far enough, or it missed
      the hole in the dark-green brick underneath.
  Note: TWO yellow axles, and this page used to say four. There are only
      two in the box.

      Yellow and black are not interchangeable here, and this page had them
      the wrong way round at first.

      Look down a hole in the green plate: it is round and smooth. Now look
      at the dark-green brick under it -- the holes in that one are
      PLUS-SHAPED. A yellow axle is a plus shape too, so it slides straight
      through the round hole and then locks into the plus-shaped one. Only
      the two holes beside the middle have a plus-shaped hole underneath,
      which is why the yellow goes there.

      A yellow axle in a round hole alone holds nothing at all -- it spins
      and slides right back out. That is exactly what makes the rotor spin
      in step 30, and it is why the blades fell off the first time we built
      this. A black peg is the opposite: it has little ridges that grip a
      round hole, so it is the right thing for the ends of the plate, where
      there is no plus-shaped hole below.

Step 34 -- rotor blades
  Sub: rotor
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Reddish Violet 11M beam | kit 45345
  Do: Line up two holes in one purple beam with the yellow axle and the
      black peg on the left, and push the beam down onto both. Do the same
      on the right with the other beam, pointing the opposite way, so their
      inner ends stop just short of each other over the middle.
  Check: The two blades form one long straight rotor, sitting level and
      floating just above the plate's studs -- beams have no bumps
      underneath, so they are held by the pegs, not by the plate. Flick a
      blade gently: the whole rotor turns, but neither blade swivels alone,
      and neither blade lifts off. It passes over the loaded seed tray at
      the back.

Step 35 -- plug in and survey
  Sub: mission
  Bin: Essential
  Add: (none -- use the red drop zone on the rainforest island)
  Do: Plug the downward Colour Sensor into port A and the seed-bay motor
      into port B. Put the red ball in the level tray. Hold one purple
      side grip in each hand and skim the skids just above the red patch.
  Check: Both plugs and cables clear the tail spine. The lens stays close
      to the target, the rotor is clear of fingers, and the seed stays
      loaded.
  Note: This is a handheld model, not a flying aircraft. Keep the tablet
      connected over Bluetooth during the whole mission.

Step 36 -- code the drop
  Sub: code
  Bin: Essential
  Add: (none)
  Do: In Icon Blocks, start a stack with the yellow "when colour" block
      set to RED on port A. Set motor B to half speed, turn it clockwise
      one quarter-turn to tip the tray, play one short effect sound until
      done, then turn motor B counterclockwise one quarter-turn.
  Check: Move the sensor over the red drop zone once. One seed rolls out
      the open end of the tray, then the empty tray returns level for
      reloading.
  Note: The seed is red too, because red is the only ball colour in the box.
      So pick the dropped seed up before the next pass -- a red ball sitting
      on white plate is a target as far as the sensor is concerned.
