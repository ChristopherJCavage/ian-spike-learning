# The UFO Case

The Spark Detectives, Module 2. Snap Circuits Jr. box (SC-100). Two cases,
one sitting each. Do Module 1 (The Night Skate Cases) first: this module
reuses its loop, its press switch and its rule "flip the motor, flip the
spin", and adds the surprise that the flip decides whether a fan flies or
just blows.

SAFETY runs through every page: goggles or sunglasses, lean back, never look
straight down at the fan, aim away from faces. Elenco's own warning for this
project says the same.

series: The Spark Detectives
module: The UFO Case

=== why
board: S2=released, launch=4
pose: coco-explain
bubble: Coco: The spinning fan pushes air DOWN. The air pushes the fan UP.
bubble: Coco: Let go, and nothing holds it on. Up it goes!
dad: Ask: what holds the fan on while it spins? (The motor turning it.) And
    when the motor stops? (Nothing.)
reason: The blade throws air down; the air pushes back up (Newton's third
    law). While the motor drives it, the blade is locked on the shaft; when
    the motor coasts, the blade unlocks and its own spin keeps lifting it.
