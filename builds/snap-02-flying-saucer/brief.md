# Brief -- The UFO Case

## Theme / story

After the Big Night Skate Show, people report a UFO over the skate park.
Sully finds a little orange disc in the grass. The Spark Detectives test the
theory the scientific way: build a launcher, guess, test. It flies: the UFO
was a fan. Next day Pip rebuilds it and it won't fly, only blow. Clue: the +
mark. Module 1's rule 4 (flip the motor, flip the spin) cracks it.

## Complexity

- Two chapters (cases), about 40 pages, one sitting each.
- Same loop as Module 1, so the build is quick; the new ideas are thrust
  (fans push air, air pushes back) and that spin direction decides fly or
  blow.
- Chris: batteries, holds the board for every launch, goggles for everyone.

## Number Lab

1 1/2 + 1 1/2 = 3 volts; 4 1/2 - 3 = 1 1/2 (why Jr parts stay on the Jr
pump); a five-flight log in inches, a bar graph, highest minus lowest
(42 - 30 = 12 as the worked example), and a fair-test question about fresh
batteries.

## Hardware

- Kit: Snap Circuits Jr. SC-100 (B1, S2, M1, the fan, three 3-snap wires, the
  big grid). 2x AA, new alkalines fly best.
- Done-when: the fan flies, Ian can say why it flies, and why turning the
  motor around stops it flying.

## Constraints

- SAFETY: goggles or sunglasses, lean back, never look straight down at the
  fan, aim away from faces, pets and lamps. Elenco's own warning for this
  project (SC-100 manual, projects 4-5) says the same.
- Jr parts on the Jr battery holder only. `check` fails M1 on B3B.
- Only parts in `kits/sc100-jr/parts.txt` (unchecked against the box).
