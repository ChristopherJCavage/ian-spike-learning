# Steps -- Hot Press

The motor sits on a red tower. Its hub turns a peg, the peg pulls a blue
beam, and the beam pushes a green plate down two black axles. The red
head under the plate comes down onto the light orange wood and lifts
again. The bricks do not change. Red means hot. Nothing gets hot.

Left and right are as you look at the press from the front, where the
motor's face looks at you.

```
Step 1 -- the table
  Sub: table
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x White 16x16 plate | kit 45345
  Do: Put the big white plate on the table, studs up.
  Check: It sits flat. The whole press goes on this.

Step 2 -- the bottom hot plate
  Sub: bed
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Red 2x4 brick with cross holes | kit 45345
  Do: Press it in the middle of the white plate, long side going left
      to right.
  Check: This is the bottom of the press. Red means hot. It is not hot.

Step 3 -- the old wood
  Sub: bed
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Flame Yellowish Orange 2x4 plate with holes | kit 45345
  Do: Stack both light orange plates on the red brick, lined up with it.
  Check: Two plates, one on the other. This is the old wood. It is
      thick.

Step 4 -- the guide sockets
  Sub: guides
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Dark Green round brick with a cross hole | kit 45345
  Do: Press one round brick at each end of the red brick, touching it.
  Check: All three sit in one row. The cross holes point up.

Step 5 -- the guide axles
  Sub: guides
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black 8M axle | kit 45345
  Do: Push a black axle down into each round brick.
  Check: Both stand straight up. Tug one gently. The cross hole grips
      it.

Step 6 -- the press plate
  Sub: press
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Green 2x8 plate with holes | kit 45345
  Do: Slide the green plate down onto both axles, through the hole at
      each end.
  Check: It slides up and down. Round holes let it move.

Step 7 -- the press head
  Sub: press
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Red 2x4 brick with cross holes | kit 45345
  Do: Lift the green plate off. Press the red brick onto its underside,
      in the middle, long side along the plate. Slide the plate back on.
  Check: The red brick hangs under the plate, right over the wood. This
      is the hot press head.

Step 8 -- the hinge
  Sub: press
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Red 1x2 plate with two holes on top | kit 45345
  Do: Press it on the green plate's front edge, in the middle. The two
      holes point at you.
  Check: Its lower hole sits just above the green plate.

Step 9 -- the tower
  Sub: tower
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 3x Bright Red 2x2x3 brick | kit 45345
  Do: Behind the red brick, skip two rows of studs. Stack the three tall
      red bricks there, in the middle.
  Check: The red tower is taller than the black axles.

Step 10 -- the motor mount
  Sub: tower
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Reddish Violet 1x4 Technic brick | kit 45345
       1x Bright Orange 1x4 brick | kit 45345
  Do: On top of the tower, press the purple brick on the front row and
      the orange brick on the back row. Each one sticks out one stud on
      both sides.
  Check: The purple brick's three holes point at you.

Step 11 -- the cap
  Sub: tower
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Red 2x4 tile | kit 45345
  Do: Press the smooth red tile on top, over both bricks.
  Check: The purple and orange bricks are locked together now.

Step 12 -- the motor pegs
  Sub: motor
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45345
  Do: Push a black peg into the purple brick's left hole and its right
      hole. Leave the middle hole empty.
  Check: Two pegs stick out toward you.

Step 13 -- the motor
  Sub: motor
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x White Small Angular Motor | kit 45345
  Do: Turn the motor so its round hub faces you and its wire comes out
      of the top. On its back, find the two holes level with the hub.
      Push those onto the two pegs.
  Check: The motor's back is flat on the tower, high over the green
      plate. It does not wobble.

Step 14 -- the crank peg and the hinge peg
  Sub: crank
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45345
  Do: Turn the hub until one of its four holes is at the very top. Push
      a peg into that hole. Push the other peg into the lower hole of
      the red hinge.
  Check: Both pegs point at you. The top peg moves when the hub turns.

Step 15 -- the rod
  Sub: crank
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Blue 7M beam | kit 45345
  Do: Hang the blue beam's top hole on the hub peg. Lift the green plate
      until the beam's bottom hole slides onto the hinge peg.
  Check: The blue beam stands straight up. The green plate hangs from
      it, high over the wood. The press is open.

Step 16 -- the face
  Sub: face
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45345
       2x White 2x2 round eye tile | kit 45345
  Do: Push a peg into each of the two holes on the motor's front, high
      up over the hub. Press an eye onto the end of each peg.
  Check: Two big eyes look at you. The hub is the mouth.

Step 17 -- the lights
  Sub: face
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45345
       1x Transparent Red rock crystal | kit 45345
       1x Transparent Bright Green rock crystal | kit 45345
  Do: Push a peg into each of the two holes on top of the motor, one on
      each side of the wire. Press the red crystal onto the right peg and
      the green crystal onto the left peg.
  Check: Red will mean hot. Green will mean done.

Step 18 -- the super wood
  Sub: garden
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Flame Yellowish Orange 2x4 plate with holes | kit 45345
  Do: Press one more light orange plate on the white plate, in front, on
      the right.
  Check: One plate, half as thick as the old wood. This is the super
      wood that comes out of the press.

Step 19 -- a frog and a bush
  Sub: garden
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Dark Green frog | kit 45345
       1x Dark Green bush | kit 45345
  Do: Stand the frog in front, on the left, looking at you. Put the bush
      at the back, on the right.
  Check: The bush is the tree nobody had to cut down.

Step 20 -- the grass
  Sub: garden
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Green grass stem | kit 45345
  Do: Put one grass stem at the back on the left, and one in the front
      corner on the right.
  Check: The press stands in a little garden.

Step 21 -- try it by hand
  Sub: try
  Bin: Essential
  Add: (none)
  Do: Turn the motor's hub slowly with your fingers, one whole turn.
  Check: The blue beam pushes the green plate down. The red head comes
      down onto the wood and stops just over it. Then it goes back up.
      Fingers stay out from under the red head.

Step 22 -- the program
  Sub: code
  Bin: Essential
  Add: (none -- the Hub and the light matrix stay out of the model)
  Do: Set the yellow Hub beside the press. Plug the motor into port B
      and the 3x3 light matrix into port A. Stand the matrix in front,
      lights toward you. Turn the hub until the press is open. Tap the
      yellow play button.
  Check: The matrix turns red, the press comes down on the wood, a sound
      plays, the press goes back up, and the matrix turns green. Now
      swap the old wood for the super wood.
```
