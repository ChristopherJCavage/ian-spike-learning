# Preflight -- Hot Press

`ldrcheck check` and `ldrcheck steps` are both at 0 problems. These are
the things they cannot see.

- **Start with the press open.** The program turns half a turn down and
  half a turn up from wherever the hub is. Turn the hub by hand until the
  crank peg is at the top before tapping play.
- **Push every peg all the way in.** New friction pegs are stiff. A crank
  peg that is only half in lets the blue beam walk off it.
- **The head does not touch the wood.** It stops 1.6 mm above the studs.
  The plates do not get thinner. Say that out loud before anyone decides
  the model is broken.
- **Nothing else goes under the head.** Two plates of wood fit. A brick
  under there stops the motor partway down, and the program waits.
- **Nothing is hot and nothing is soaked.** Red bricks, the red crystal,
  and the red matrix mean heat. They are not heat.
- **Fingers.** The press comes down. Hands stay off the green plate and
  out from under the red head while it runs.
- **The eyes and the crystals turn on their pegs.** That is fine. They
  are decoration. The eyes look blank in the LeoCAD pictures because
  LDraw has no eye print. The real tiles have the big cartoon eye.
- **Press the tower bricks down hard.** The motor hangs off the front of
  the tower. The tile on top holds the purple and orange bricks together,
  and the studs hold the rest.
