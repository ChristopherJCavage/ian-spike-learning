# Steps -- The Night Skate Cases

Build copy for each `step` and `scene` in `circuit.snap`. The comic
(`deck.md`) puts a character and a speech bubble beside each one; this file
is the instruction itself. `python3 tools/snap.py check` fails if this file
and the layout disagree about which steps and scenes exist.

`Say` is the big line on the page, read aloud. `Do` is for the grown-up.
`Check` is what to look for before the next page. `Ask` is a question for Ian.

Every block has its name printed on it (B3B, S1, L4, and a number on each
blue wire). Pages show that name big, because Elenco says colours and styles
may vary. On the Beginner grid the numbers 1-7 run across the top and the
letters A-E run down the side. A post is the number, then the letter: 1A is
the top-left. Line parts up by their metal snaps, not by the edge of the
plastic. The green switch board is longer than its two snaps.

Step 1 -- The Ground
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x mini base grid | kit scb20
  Say: Lay the clear board flat. Find the corner post 1A.
  Do: Mini grid on the table, long side left to right. Numbers 1-7 across
      the top, letters A-E down the left. No batteries in anything yet.
  Check: Ian can point to 1A, then to 3D.

Step 2 -- The Pump
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x battery holder B3B | kit scb20
  Say: The battery holder hangs off the top. Snaps on 1A and 3A.
  Do: You put 3 AA batteries in B3B first (flat end on the spring, spring
      pressed straight back). The clear case cannot lie on the row. It hangs
      off the top edge, and only the two snaps touch 1A and 3A. Plus is 1A.
  Check: The holder is off the board. Snaps are on 1A and 3A.

Step 3 -- The Gate
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x slide switch S1 | kit scb20
  Say: Switch snaps on 1C and 1D. Slide it to OFF.
  Do: S1 has two metal snaps, one grid step apart: 1C and 1D. The green
      board is longer than that. Ignore the plastic edge and seat the snaps.
      Lever to OFF before anything else goes on.
  Check: Snaps on 1C and 1D, lever OFF.

Step 4 -- The Glow Wheel
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x lamp L4 | kit scb20
  Say: Lamp snaps on 3D and 3C.
  Do: L4 covers 3D and 3C. The lamp has no right or wrong way round.
  Check: The lamp's snaps are on 3D and 3C.

Step 5 -- First slide tube
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x 3-snap wire | kit scb20
  Say: Blue tube from 1A down to 1C.
  Do: The wire lies down column 1, 1A to 1C, on top of the battery snap and
      the switch's upper snap.
  Check: The tube's snaps are 1A, 1B and 1C.

Step 6 -- Second slide tube
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x 3-snap wire | kit scb20
  Say: Blue tube from 1D across to 3D.
  Do: Along row D, 1D to 3D, on top of the switch's lower snap and the
      lamp's lower snap.
  Check: The tube's snaps are 1D, 2D and 3D.

Step 7 -- Close the circle
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x 3-snap wire | kit scb20
  Say: Last tube, from 3C up to 3A.
  Do: Up column 3, 3C to 3A. Keep the switch OFF until the next page.
  Check: Tubes are 1A-1C, 1D-3D and 3A-3C. The lamp is dark because the
      switch is off.

Step 8 -- The Bulb
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x egg | kit scb20
  Say: Press the white ball onto the yellow lamp.
  Do: The egg snaps on L4. It is the bulb you see. It can also sit on the
      LED later; lift it off in Case 2 when you want to see the LED itself.
  Check: The white ball is on the yellow lamp.

Scene watch -- Open the gate!
  Say: Slide the Gate ON. Watch the water go all the way round!
  Do: Ian slides S1 to ON. If the lamp stays dark, press every tube end down
      hard, then check the batteries.
  Check: The Glow Wheel lights up.

Scene red -- Red light
  Say: Gate ON. What colour is the light?
  Do: Slide S1 on. In a dim room the colour is easier to see.
  Check: D10 glows red. (If it glows yellow, your D10's + end is the other way
      from our drawing; the answers on the next pages swap. Tell Chris.)

Scene lantern -- The party lantern
  Say: Egg on top, Gate ON. A glowing party lantern!
  Do: The egg just sits on top of D10. Turn the lights down.
  Check: The egg glows.

Scene silent -- Pip's horn
  Say: Push the Button Gate and hold it. Listen!
  Do: Ian presses S2. With the horn in Pip's way round, nothing happens.
  Check: Silence. That is the mystery. (If it honks, your W1 is marked the
      other way round from ours: just turn it so it is silent, and carry on.)

Scene honk -- HONK!
  Say: Push and hold the Button Gate. HONK!
  Do: The horn is loud. Short presses are plenty.
  Check: It honks while the button is down, and stops when Ian lets go.

Scene spin -- The Glow Spinner
  Say: Gate ON. It spins AND it glows!
  Do: Faces back from the fan. Never touch it while it spins.
  Check: The fan spins and the motor lights up.
