# Steps -- The Night Skate Cases

Build copy for each `step` and `scene` in `circuit.snap`. The comic
(`deck.md`) puts a character and a speech bubble beside each one; this file
is the instruction itself. `python3 tools/snap.py check` fails if this file
and the layout disagree about which steps and scenes exist, or if a post
named here is not a snap on that page's board.

`Say` is the big line on the page, read aloud. `Do` is for the grown-up.
`Check` is what to look for before the next page. `Ask` is a question for Ian.

Every block has its name printed on it (B3B, S1, D10, and a number on each
blue wire). Pages show that name big, because Elenco says colours and styles
may vary. On the Beginner grid the numbers 1-7 run across the top and the
letters A-E run down the side. A post is the number, then the letter: 1A is
the top-left. Every Beginner block is three posts long with a metal snap at
each end; the blue wires have a third snap in the middle. The battery
holder is 3x3 and only one edge has snaps, so it rides the edge of the grid.

Step 1 -- The Ground
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x mini base grid | kit scb20
  Say: Lay the clear board flat. Find the corner post 1A.
  Do: Mini grid on the table, long side left to right. Numbers 1-7 across
      the top, letters A-E down the left. No batteries in anything yet.
  Check: Ian can point to 1A, then to 3E.

Step 2 -- The Pump
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x battery holder B3B | kit scb20
  Say: The battery holder rides the top edge. Snaps on 1A and 3A.
  Do: You put 3 AA batteries in B3B first (flat end on the spring). Its two
      snaps are on one edge only: + and -. Snap that edge onto row A, + on
      1A, - on 3A. The rest of the holder, batteries and all, hangs off the
      top of the grid.
  Check: Snaps on 1A (+) and 3A. The batteries are off the board.

Step 3 -- The Gate
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x slide switch S1 | kit scb20
  Say: The switch snaps on 1C and 1E. Slide it to OFF.
  Do: S1 runs down column 1 from 1C to 1E. The middle of it is plastic, not
      a snap. Lever to OFF before anything else goes on.
  Check: Snaps on 1C and 1E, lever OFF.

Step 4 -- The Colour Wheel
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x colour light D10 | kit scb20
  Say: The light snaps on 3C and 3E. The + goes at the top, on 3C.
  Do: D10 runs down column 3 from 3C to 3E. Find the little + on one end
      and put that end on 3C.
  Check: D10's + is on 3C, its other snap on 3E.

Step 5 -- First slide tube
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x 3-snap wire | kit scb20
  Say: Blue tube from 1A down to 1C.
  Do: The wire lies down column 1, on top of the holder's + snap (1A) and
      the switch's top snap (1C).
  Check: The tube's snaps are 1A, 1B and 1C.

Step 6 -- Second slide tube
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x 3-snap wire | kit scb20
  Say: Blue tube from 1E across to 3E.
  Do: Along row E, on top of the switch's bottom snap (1E) and the light's
      bottom snap (3E).
  Check: The tube's snaps are 1E, 2E and 3E.

Step 7 -- Close the circle
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x 3-snap wire | kit scb20
  Say: Last tube, from 3C up to 3A.
  Do: Up column 3, on top of the light's + snap (3C) and the holder's -
      snap (3A). Keep the switch OFF until the next page.
  Check: Tubes are 1A-1C, 1E-3E and 3C-3A. The light is dark because the
      switch is off.

Step 8 -- The Bulb
  Bin: Beginner
  Add: 1x egg | kit scb20
  Say: Press the white ball onto the light.
  Do: The egg sits on the middle of D10, between the two tube ends. It is
      the bulb you see. Lift it off when you need to turn D10 around.
  Check: The white ball is on D10.

Scene watch -- Open the gate!
  Say: Slide the Gate ON. Watch the water go all the way round!
  Do: Ian slides S1 to ON. If the light stays dark, press every tube end
      down hard, then check the batteries.
  Check: The ball glows yellow.

Scene red -- Red light
  Say: Gate ON. What colour is the light now?
  Do: D10 is turned around: + on 3E. Slide S1 on. A dim room helps.
  Check: The ball glows red. (If it glows yellow, your D10's + end is the
      other way from our drawing; the answers on the next pages swap. Tell
      Chris.)

Scene silent -- The horn that works
  Say: Push the Button Gate and hold it. HONK!
  Do: The + snap is on 3E, the bottom snap, on the blue tube. Ian presses S2.
  Check: It honks while the button is down. If it is silent, turn W1 so the
      + is on 3E.

Scene honk -- HONK!
  Say: Push and hold the Button Gate. HONK!
  Do: The horn is loud. Short presses are plenty.
  Check: It honks while the button is down, and stops when Ian lets go.

Scene c4-both -- Red and white
  Say: Gate ON. Red AND white!
  Do: D10 + on 3E with the egg. The white light L4 stands on 5C and 5E. One
      tube runs 3E-5E, one runs 3C-5C, each snapped on top at 3E or 3C.
  Check: Gate ON lights both. Gate OFF darkens both. Pull the 3C-5C tube
      and only the white light goes out.
