Plug the motor into port A, the force sensor into port B and the light matrix into port C. Hold the base with one hand. With the other, push Leo up toward one lip, slowly -- the motor turns, stiffly -- then back down and up toward the other lip. Watch the grey gears.

Check: Leo goes up each side. Both gears roll along the teeth of their rails the whole way, turning as they go, and nothing else touches the ramp. The cables do not catch on the arm.
