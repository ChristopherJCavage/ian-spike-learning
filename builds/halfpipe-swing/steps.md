# Steps -- Half-pipe Swing

Human connection language. Numbers **must** match `0 STEP` in `model.ldr`
(LeoCAD step 1 = first `0 STEP`). Template: `context/connection-language.md`.

Three boxes. Every step says which bin: **Essential** (white baseplates,
green and purple bricks, Leo, the orange board and plate, the light
matrix), **Prime** (hub, motor, force sensor, beams, pegs, gears, the
yellow and black axles), **Expansion** (the four curved racks, the red
axles, the yellow half bushes).

"Front" is where you sit. Leo will face you, and the motor's round disc
points at you. "Left" and "right" are your left and right, sitting
there -- even on the pages where the picture is taken from behind.

Step 1 -- baseplates
  Sub: base
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x White 16x16 plate, 1x White 8x16 plate | kit 45345
  Do: Lay the big square plate in front of you. Lay the long plate
      behind it, long side left-to-right, edges touching.
  Check: Two white plates make one big rectangle, longer front-to-back
      than side-to-side.

Step 2 -- long green bricks
  Sub: base
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Green 1x16 Technic brick | kit 45345
  Do: Press one green brick across the big plate on the 11th row of
      studs, counting from the edge nearest you. Press the other across
      the long plate on its very last row, the edge furthest from you.
  Check: Both green bricks run left-to-right, end to end of their
      plates, holes facing you.

Step 3 -- the motor
  Sub: frame
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 1x White Large Motor | kit 45678
  Do: Pick up the Large Motor. Hold it with the round disc facing you
      and the cable end pointing down. It is shown floating where it
      will go; keep it in your hand for the next two pages.
  Check: You are looking straight at the round disc. The cable socket
      is at the bottom.

Step 4 -- six pegs, all pointing at you
  Sub: frame
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 6x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45678
  Do: Each side of the disc has a column of three holes. Push a peg
      into the bottom two holes of each column -- four pegs. Then push
      two pegs into the front green brick, each two holes out from the
      middle.
  Check: Four pegs stick out of the motor toward you, two each side of
      the disc. Two pegs stick out of the green brick, the same
      distance apart as the motor's left and right pegs.

Step 5 -- posts
  Sub: frame
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 2x Black 9M beam | kit 45678
  Do: Push a black beam onto the two pegs on each side of the motor,
      holes facing you, so it hangs straight down. Then lower the
      whole motor onto the green brick so each beam's bottom hole
      lands on a brick peg.
  Check: The motor stands on two black legs, disc facing you, with a
      finger's gap under it. Nothing wobbles.

Step 6 -- crossbeam pegs
  Sub: frame
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 4x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45678
  Do: On the front of each post, count down from the top: push a peg
      into hole 6 and hole 8.
  Check: Four pegs stick out toward you below the motor, two per post,
      at the same two heights.

Step 7 -- crossbeams
  Sub: frame
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 2x Medium Azur 13M beam | kit 45678
  Do: Press one blue beam across each pair of pegs, centred, so the
      same number of holes sticks out each side.
  Check: Two blue beams run left-to-right under the motor, one above
      the other, each with four holes free beyond each post.

Step 8 -- hub pegs
  Sub: hub
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 4x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45678
  Do: Turn the model around. Push four pegs into the back of the motor,
      the same four holes as on the front.
  Check: Four pegs stick out of the motor's back, away from the disc.

Step 9 -- hub
  Sub: hub
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 1x Bright Yellow Prime Hub | kit 45678
  Do: Lay the hub flat, lights facing UP, and push one short end onto
      the four pegs.
  Check: The hub sticks straight out behind the motor, flat, with the
      5x5 lights on top. Its far end is in the air for now.

Step 10 -- leg pegs
  Sub: hub
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 6x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45678
  Do: Push four pegs into the hub's far short end, the same pattern as
      before. Push two pegs into the back green brick, two holes out
      from the middle, on the side facing away from you.
  Check: Six pegs all point away from the motor -- four out of the hub,
      two out of the back green brick right under them.

Step 11 -- rear legs
  Sub: hub
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 2x Black 9M beam | kit 45678
  Do: Each leg takes three pegs at once: two from the hub, one from the
      green brick. Line both legs up, then push them on together.
  Check: The hub sits level on its two back legs. Press on the hub --
      it does not sag.

Step 12 -- button riser
  Sub: button
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Reddish Violet 1x4 Technic brick | kit 45345
  Do: Press a purple brick onto the LEFT end of the front green brick
      (your left), flush with its end.
  Check: The purple brick sits on the last four studs on your left. In
      the picture, taken from behind, that is the right-hand end.

Step 13 -- button pegs
  Sub: button
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 2x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45678
  Do: Push two pegs into the BACK of the purple brick, its two outer
      holes.
  Check: Two pegs stick out of the purple brick toward the hub.

Step 14 -- force sensor
  Sub: button
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 1x White Force Sensor | kit 45678
  Do: Stand the force sensor on end, button UP, and push it onto the two
      pegs using two holes on one flat side.
  Check: The button points at the ceiling, behind the motor on your
      left. It does not touch the baseplate.

Step 15 -- disc pegs
  Sub: rider
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 2x Bright Blue 3M peg | kit 45678
  Do: First turn the disc by hand until the little mark on it lines up
      with the mark on the motor -- that is where the program's
      "position 0" is. Then push two long blue pegs into the disc: the
      hole straight above the middle and the hole straight below it.
      Short end in the disc, long end toward you.
  Check: Two blue pegs stick out of the disc, one above the other.

Step 16 -- offset beam
  Sub: rider
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 1x Bright Reddish Violet 5M beam | kit 45678
  Do: Push the purple beam onto the two blue pegs by its 1st and 3rd
      holes, so it hangs straight down from the disc.
  Check: A short purple beam hangs from the disc, in front of the blue
      crossbeams, two holes free below the pegs.

Step 17 -- arm pegs
  Sub: rider
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 2x Bright Blue 3M peg | kit 45678
  Do: Push two more long blue pegs into the purple beam's bottom two
      holes, short end in the beam, long end toward you.
  Check: Two blue pegs stick out of the bottom of the purple beam.

Step 18 -- arm
  Sub: rider
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 1x Medium Azur 7M beam | kit 45678
  Do: Push the blue 7M beam onto those two pegs by its 2nd and 3rd
      holes, so it hangs straight down.
  Check: The arm hangs one step in front of the purple beam and reaches
      much further down. Turn the disc by hand: everything swings
      together.

Step 19 -- T-beam pegs
  Sub: rider
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 2x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45678
  Do: Push two pegs into the front of the arm: the second- and third-
      from-bottom holes.
  Check: Two pegs stick out near the bottom of the arm, the very bottom
      hole left empty.

Step 20 -- T-beam
  Sub: rider
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 1x Black T-beam | kit 45678
  Do: Push the T-beam onto those two pegs, stem up, crossbar at the
      bottom.
  Check: An upside-down T at the end of the arm. The middle hole of its
      crossbar lines up with the arm's empty bottom hole.

Step 21 -- board pegs
  Sub: rider
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 2x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45678
  Do: Push a peg into each END hole of the crossbar, pointing at you.
  Check: Two pegs, side by side, the middle hole empty between them.

Step 22 -- board brick
  Sub: rider
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Reddish Violet 1x4 Technic brick | kit 45345
  Do: Push the purple brick onto both pegs, studs UP.
  Check: Four studs on top, level, at the bottom of the arm. Look
      through the brick's middle hole: you can see straight through the
      T-beam and the arm.

Step 23 -- skateboard
  Sub: rider
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Flame Yellowish Orange board | kit 45345
  Do: Press the orange board onto the purple brick, long way
      left-to-right, its two studs on top.
  Check: The board sits flat and does not tip either way.

Step 24 -- Leo, legs and body
  Sub: rider
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Dark Green short legs, 1x Bright Blue torso | kit 45345
  Do: Snap Leo's short green legs onto the board's two studs, facing
      you. Press his blue torso on top.
  Check: Leo stands on the board facing you, his back against the
      T-beam.

Step 25 -- Leo, head and hair
  Sub: rider
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Yellow head, 1x Reddish Brown wig | kit 45345
  Do: Press on his head, then his brown hair.
  Check: Leo is complete and grinning. The board is level.

Step 26 -- gear axle and back gear
  Sub: gears
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 1x Medium Stone Grey Z28 gear, 1x Bright Yellow 7M axle | kit 45678
  Do: Hold a grey gear behind the arm, at the bottom. With the other
      hand, push the yellow axle in from the front, under the board:
      through the middle hole of the purple brick, the T-beam and the
      arm, and into the gear.
  Check: Spin the gear with a finger. The gear and the axle turn
      together, freely, and the board and Leo stay still.

Step 27 -- half bushes
  Sub: gears
  Bin: Expansion
  Add: 3x Bright Yellow 1/2 bush | kit 45681
  Do: Push one half bush onto the back end of the axle, behind the gear.
      Slide two onto the front end, all the way back against the purple
      brick.
  Check: The back gear cannot slide off. Two yellow rings sit in front
      of the brick, with some axle still sticking out past them.

Step 28 -- front gear
  Sub: gears
  Bin: Prime / Expansion
  Add: 1x Medium Stone Grey Z28 gear | kit 45678 ; 1x Bright Yellow 1/2 bush | kit 45681
  Do: Slide the second grey gear onto the front of the axle, against
      the two half bushes. Push the last half bush on in front of it.
  Check: One gear behind the arm, one in front of Leo, both on the same
      yellow axle. Spin one and the other spins with it.

Step 29 -- rail pegs
  Sub: ramp
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 2x Bright Blue 3M peg | kit 45678
  Do: Push a long blue peg into each END hole of the UPPER blue beam,
      short end in the beam, long end pointing at you.
  Check: Two long blue pegs, far left and far right of the upper blue
      beam.

Step 30 -- back rail
  Sub: ramp
  Bin: Expansion / Prime
  Add: 2x Black curved rack | kit 45681 ; 1x Black 8M axle | kit 45678
  Do: Turn Leo all the way over to your LEFT and hold him there. Hang
      the first curved rack on the peg on your RIGHT, teeth inside, its
      thin end tab at the bottom. Now turn Leo slowly over to the right
      -- the back gear rolls onto the rack's teeth -- and hold him there.
      Turn the second rack over and hang it on the peg on your left so
      its thin tab lies IN FRONT of the first one's.
      Push the black axle through both tabs from the front, all the way
      into the middle hole of the lower blue beam.
  Check: A U-shaped rail behind Leo, teeth on the inside, with the back
      gear sitting in its teeth at the bottom. At the bottom the two
      tabs overlap to make one full-thickness end, and the black axle
      sticks out toward you.

Step 31 -- lip axles
  Sub: ramp
  Bin: Expansion
  Add: 2x Bright Red 6M axle | kit 45681
  Do: Push a red axle through the hole at each top end of the rail,
      from the front. Leave most of it sticking out toward you.
  Check: Three axles stick out toward you: a red one at each lip, the
      black one at the bottom.

Step 32 -- front rail
  Sub: ramp
  Bin: Expansion
  Add: 2x Black curved rack | kit 45681
  Do: Same two pieces, the same way round, slid onto the three axles
      from the front: turn Leo to your left and slide on the RIGHT one
      first; turn him to your right and slide on the turned-over one on
      the left, tabs overlapping at the bottom.
  Check: Two rails, back and front, with Leo between them and a gear in
      each rail's teeth. Look along the ramp from the side -- the two
      rails line up.

Step 33 -- light stand
  Sub: lights
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Flame Yellowish Orange 2x4 plate with holes, 2x Bright Reddish Violet 1x4 Technic brick | kit 45345
  Do: Press the orange plate onto the middle of the big plate's front
      edge, long way left-to-right, so its back row of studs is on the
      plate and its front row hangs out over the table. Stack the two
      purple bricks on its front row, holes facing you.
  Check: A little purple tower stands in front of the ramp, just past
      the edge of the white plate, not touching the front rail.

Step 34 -- light pegs
  Sub: lights
  Bin: Prime
  Add: 2x Black connector peg (friction) | kit 45678
  Do: Push two pegs into the front of the TOP purple brick, its two
      outer holes.
  Check: Two pegs stick out toward you from the top of the tower.

Step 35 -- light matrix
  Sub: lights
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Medium Azur 3x3 Color Light Matrix | kit 45345
  Do: Push the light matrix onto the two pegs by the two holes on its
      back, the nine lights facing you.
  Check: Nine lights look at you from under the ramp. Its cable reaches
      round to the hub.

Step 36 -- try it with your hand (no new parts)
  Sub: code
  Do: Plug the motor into port A, the force sensor into port B and the
      light matrix into port C. Hold the base with one hand. With the
      other, push Leo up toward one lip, slowly -- the motor turns,
      stiffly -- then back down and up toward the other lip. Watch the
      grey gears.
  Check: Leo goes up each side. Both gears roll along the teeth of their
      rails the whole way, turning as they go, and nothing else touches
      the ramp. The cables do not catch on the arm.

Step 37 -- code (no new parts)
  Sub: code
  Do: Open the SPIKE App, Word Blocks. Build the program on the code
      sheet with Chris reading it out. Put Leo back at the bottom, then
      run it and press the button.
  Check: Leo drops in, swings lip to lip three times -- the front lights
      flash orange at every lip -- and lands in the middle with green
      lights, a smile and a sound.
