## Distance sensor
# Two rails run forward from the Hub's side holes, one per row (y=-80
# and y=-100, both x=+-80) so the gantry reaches the Hub through two
# non-collinear rows instead of one. Both rows are one step lower than
# the naive y=-60 four-hole row: the front wheels are solid discs
# spanning y=-70..70, so a rail at y=-60 runs its whole length flush
# against wheel material. Each rail needs its own hub pins at the row's
# z=-80 and z=80 holes (160 LDU apart) plus reach to z=200 to clear the
# wheel's z=190 face -- only the 15M beam, the longest in the kit,
# spans that; a shorter 9M beam covered the old z=40/80 holes but comes
# up short of z=-80. At z=200 a 3M pin per row hands off to a 3M
# upright, which a last pin ties into the sensor's lower hole.
