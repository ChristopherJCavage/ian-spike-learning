# Steps -- Rainforest Drop Zone

Human connection language. Numbers **must** match `0 STEP` in `model.ldr`
(LeoCAD step 1 = first `0 STEP`).

This is not a machine, it is a place: the strip of rainforest the Seed Drone
flies down. Nothing here has a motor and nothing plugs into the Hub.

Built for a 60-minute class. Twenty pieces, nine steps. It is meant to be
finished by the second child while the first is still building the drone, so
every step is one kind of piece and nothing needs lining up by eye.

Words that stay fixed. The **island** is the one white plate, stood so it is
narrow and deep. The **walls** are the two long green bricks down its long
edges. The **corridor** is the open lane between them. The **drop zone** is
the red block across the corridor -- the only red on the island, and the only
thing here the program cares about.

Step 1 -- the island
  Sub: base
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x White 8x16 plate | kit 45345
  Do: Put the long white plate on the table so it is narrow left-to-right
      and long away from you, like a road.
  Check: A white rectangle 8 studs wide and 16 studs deep, pointing away
      from you.
  Note: There are bigger white plates in the box. Leave them -- this one is
      the right size for an hour.

Step 2 -- the drop zone
  Sub: clearing
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Red 2x4 tile | kit 45345
  Do: Find the middle of the plate. Lay the two smooth red tiles side by
      side across it, both pointing left-to-right, one just in front of the
      other, so they make one solid red square with no white between them.
  Check: A red square in the middle of the plate, four studs each way, with
      two studs of white left on each side. Run a finger over it -- flat and
      smooth all the way.
  Note: This is the piece the program is about. The drone looks down, sees
      red, and drops the seed. Everything else is scenery.

      TWO tiles, and this page used to say three. There are only two Bright
      Red 2x4 tiles in the box. The green walls in the next step move in to
      match, so the red still reaches wall to wall.

      Smooth tiles, not plates with studs. Studs cast little shadows and the
      sensor reads a shadow as a darker colour.

Step 3 -- the walls
  Sub: forest
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Green 1x16 Technic brick | kit 45345
  Do: Lay one long green brick down each side of the red, running the whole
      way from front to back. Leave ONE row of white studs showing outside
      each wall -- the walls do not go on the very last row.
  Check: Two green walls with a lane between them, and one bare white row
      outside each wall. The red square now reaches from one wall to the
      other, touching both.
  Note: This is the forest closing in, and it is the reason the drop zone is
      easy to hit: the drone flies straight down the lane, and the red goes
      all the way across it. There is no aiming left and right to get wrong.

      The walls also stop a seed rolling off the table.

Step 4 -- the trees
  Sub: forest
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Dark Green round 2x2 brick, 2x Dark Green bush | kit 45345
  Do: Press one round dark green brick onto the middle of the plate at each
      end of the lane, then push a spiky bush down onto each one.
  Check: Two trees, one at each end, both in the middle of the lane. Look
      down the lane from one end -- the other tree is straight ahead and the
      red band is between them. That is the flight path.
  Note: One brick under each bush, not two. A short tree is steadier if the
      table gets knocked, and it leaves more room over the top for the
      drone's blades.

      The leaves are much wider than the trunk and hang out past the ends of
      the plate. Real trees do that.

Step 5 -- the ranger
  Sub: ranger
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Orange minifigure legs, 1x Bright Green minifigure torso | kit 45345
  Do: Stand the orange legs in the lane between the red band and the far
      tree, facing you. Push the green body down onto the legs.
  Check: A figure with no head yet, standing on its own, facing you, with
      the red band in front of her feet.
  Note: Push down until both feet are properly on studs. If she leans, she
      is half on and half off a stud -- lift her and try again one stud over.

Step 6 -- her head
  Sub: ranger
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Bright Yellow minifigure head, 1x Medium Nougat wig | kit 45345
  Do: Push the head down onto the neck, then press the hair on top.
  Check: She stands without wobbling and looks down the lane at the drop
      zone.

Step 7 -- the controller
  Sub: ranger
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Black camera | kit 45345
  Do: Clip the little black camera into her hands so she is holding it up in
      front of her, like someone flying a drone.
  Check: Both hands are on it and it stays put when you turn her round.
  Note: This is the kit's camera. There is no drone controller piece and
      this is the closest thing in the box -- a small dark slab held in two
      hands. Her hands squeeze around it so it looks a bit squashed. That is
      how a minifigure hand holds anything.

Step 8 -- the frog
  Sub: animals
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 1x Dark Green frog | kit 45345
  Do: Put the frog on the white plate behind the red band, off to one side.
  Check: The frog is on the white, not the red.
  Note: Keep it off the red. Anything sitting on the drop zone is something
      the sensor might see instead of the target.

Step 9 -- the grass
  Sub: undergrowth
  Bin: Essential
  Add: 2x Bright Green grass stem | kit 45345
  Do: Push one grass tuft onto the bare white row OUTSIDE one wall, behind
      the red, and the other onto the bare row outside the other wall, in
      front of the red.
  Check: Two green tufts, both outside the walls, neither in the lane and
      neither leaning over the red.
  Note: Each tuft is a little tube that drops over ONE stud, so it has to
      land exactly on a bump. If it will not go down flat, it is between two
      studs -- slide it over.

      TWO tufts, and this page used to say four. The kit's own parts list
      carried the same stem on two rows -- design 31576 and design 2659 --
      and treated them as different pieces, so it counted four when the box
      holds two. They are one piece under a design ID and its mould revision.
      Count the bag, not the list.

Step 10 -- fly the mission
  Sub: mission
  Bin: (none -- the island is finished)
  Add: (none)
  Do: Put the finished Seed Drone in your hands, load one red ball in its
      tray, and start the program. Fly in low over one tree and straight
      down the lane, keeping the sensor under the drone pointing down.
  Check: As the sensor crosses the red, the tray tips, the ball drops on the
      drop zone, and the drone plays its sound. Lift away, and it is ready to
      go again.
  Note: If nothing happens, fly lower and slower -- the sensor has to be
      about 1 to 2 cm above the red.

      Pick the seed up before the next run. The seed is red too, so a ball
      left lying on the white plate is a target as far as the sensor is
      concerned, and the drone will drop again over it.
