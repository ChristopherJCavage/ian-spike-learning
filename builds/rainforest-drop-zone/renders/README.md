# LeoCAD PNGs

Instruction stills `step-001.png` … `step-019.png` (LeoCAD CLI, camera per
`screens.md`, previous steps faded). Step 20 has no render: the black target
is a square of card, not a LEGO part, so there is nothing for LeoCAD to
draw.

`highlight` is off for every step here. On the drone the two white
electronics vanish into a white page without it; nothing on this island has
that problem, so the fade alone carries the eye.

Check views of the finished island:

- `check-top.png` -- plan view. The one that matters: it shows the red row
  crossing the seam, the four orange corner marks framing the bay, and
  whether anything has crept into the clearing.
- `check-iso.png` -- default instruction camera 55 225
- `check-front.png` -- height check. Sight along it to see that the two
  bushes are the tallest things and that the middle is open.
- `check-left.png` -- side profile, best view of the two trees standing on
  opposite diagonal corners.

No `story-*.png` and no `program.*`. This build has no story slides and no
program.
