# Combined SPIKE Electronics -- Essential 45345 + Prime 45678
#                              + Prime Expansion 45681
#
# The whole reason these kits are compatible: SPIKE Essential and
# SPIKE Prime run the same SPIKE App and the same underlying hub firmware
# family. Motors, sensors, and cables from any box plug into either
# hub. This file is the one place to check "what electronics do we own
# across all three bins" before starting a build.
#
# The Expansion 45681 is an ADD-ON: no hub, no battery, no USB cable.
# It contributes exactly two electronic parts, both duplicates of things
# we already had -- so it raises counts, it does not add capability.
#
# Format (pipe-delimited, grep-friendly):
#   QTY | PART | FROM KIT | NOTES
#
# Example: grep -i "sensor" combined-electronics.txt

## Hubs (1 per kit -- pick ONE hub per build, they are not daisy-chained)
1 | Hub, Small (SPIKE Essential Hub, element 100885/106060) | 45345 Essential | 2 ports (A and B), no built-in light matrix -- the 3x3 Colour Light Matrix is a separate accessory that uses one port. Good default for Ian's Saturday class
1 | Hub, Prime (SPIKE Prime Hub, element 105474) | 45678 Prime | 6 ports, built-in 5x5 light matrix + speaker + light/IMU sensors on board -- more powerful, use once he's ready to graduate

## Motors
2 | Small Angular Motor (element 68488, "MOTOR, NO. 14") | 45345 Essential | Lower torque, higher speed -- good for wheels/spinners on simple builds
1 | Medium Angular Motor (element 99085, "MOTOR, NO. 11") | 45678 Prime | Mid torque/speed, built-in rotation sensor
1 | Medium Angular Motor (element 99085, "MOTOR, NO. 11") | 45681 Expansion | Second, identical unit -- 2 medium total
2 | Large Motor (element 79818, "MOTOR, NO. 12") | 45678 Prime | Highest torque, built-in rotation sensor -- best for driving wheels or lifting arms
TOTAL: 6 motors across all three kits (2 small, 2 medium, 2 large)
NOTE: the Prime hub has 6 ports, so all 6 motors can run at once only if
nothing else is plugged in. Any sensor takes a port away from a motor.

## Sensors
1 | Color Sensor (element 37308, "SENSOR COLOUR, NO. 2") | 45345 Essential | Also detects reflected light intensity
1 | Color Sensor (element 37308, "SENSOR COLOUR, NO. 2") | 45678 Prime | Second, identical unit
1 | Color Sensor (element 37308, "SENSOR COLOUR, NO. 2") | 45681 Expansion | Third, identical unit -- 3 total across all three kits
1 | Force Sensor (element 37312, "SENSOR FORCE, NO. 1") | 45678 Prime | 0-10 Newton, also works as a simple button/touch sensor
1 | Distance Sensor (element 37316, "SENSOR DISTANCE, NO. 1") | 45678 Prime | Ultrasonic, has 4 built-in lights around the "eyes"
1 | 3x3 Color Light Matrix (element 47592, "LIGHT, NO. 1") | 45345 Essential | Not a sensor -- a 3x3 RGB display accessory, plugs into a hub port like a sensor

## Power & Cables
1 | Rechargeable Battery (element 100887) | 45345 Essential | Fits the Essential hub
1 | Rechargeable Battery (element 107826) | 45678 Prime | Fits the Prime hub (batteries are NOT interchangeable between hub models)
1 | USB Cable, USB-A to Micro-B (element 39881) | 45345 Essential | Charges either hub, also used for firmware updates / wired programming
1 | USB Cable, USB-A to Micro-B (element 39881) | 45678 Prime | Second, identical cable -- 2 total across both kits

# --- Practical notes ---
# - Each hub charges/pairs independently. You cannot mix-and-match a
#   Prime battery into an Essential hub or vice versa -- check the plug
#   shape before assuming a swap will work.
# - Every motor and sensor uses the same 6-wire flat connector and works
#   on any port of either hub, regardless of which box it came from.
# - The SPIKE App auto-detects whichever hub you connect and shows the
#   matching block palette (Essential's simplified Word/Icon Blocks vs
#   Prime's fuller Word Blocks / Python), so there's no separate app to
#   install for either kit. Essential streams; Prime downloads. Block
#   shape, the motor multi-select, and the Extensions tray are documented
#   in context/spike-blocks.md.
