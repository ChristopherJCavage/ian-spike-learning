# Site content -- stem.galaxymoose.com

Source copy for the public site. One file per page. The Figma file
(`3Wu7Pq7YolVBz6HhwCkhL5`) and the static build both read from here; if the
words differ, these files win.

## House rules for the words

- First person. Chris is talking. Contractions are fine.
- Title Case for nav items, page and section headings, panel titles,
  eyebrows, and button labels ("Kits", "How It's Made", "Click
  Through the Steps"). Lowercase only short stop words in the middle (a,
  an, the, and, or, for, of, to, in, on, by, at, with). Verbs stay
  capitalized ("Is", "Was"). Filenames stay as written ("ldrcheck Asks
  the Boring Questions"). Body copy, the footer disclaimer, print notes,
  and troubleshooting bullets are ordinary sentences.
- `LEGO®` gets the ® on every use, is an adjective ("LEGO® bricks", never
  "LEGOs"), sits in the running text in the same typeface, and is never in
  a heading eyebrow or set in caps on its own. `SPIKE™` likewise.
- The red LEGO logo never appears. No official box photos, catalog photos,
  or scans of official instructions. Every picture is our own LeoCAD render
  or our own photo of our own model (see `photos.md`).
- Say each thing once. The retirement story lives in full on Kits; other
  pages get one sentence and a link. Heroes are one line plus one or two
  sentences; the mechanism goes in How It Works further down. The home page
  is the exception: a short hero paragraph, then the Why section.
- Long-form pages (About, How It's Made, Legal) are one column, about
  680px wide, with pictures inline. No side-by-side panels for prose.
- American spelling on the site ("color"), even though the repo's build
  files use "colour".
- Dates: company sales of SPIKE Essential and Prime ended June 30, 2026.
  The SPIKE App is supported through June 30, 2031. Don't round either.
- Ian is first name and age only. Photos may show his hands, never his
  face. The coach and my wife are not named.
- The site doesn't name the club. Call it "the school's LEGO® robotics
  club", don't guess at a name, and don't write "FIRST" -- that is a
  separate trademark.
- Club-Ready means `venue: class` and only kits the club owns, about one
  session, written so a room of six-year-olds can finish it. Today that
  list is just set 45345. Home Build means more than one box, or a kit the
  club doesn't have, and a grown-up does the hard part. Class is something
  a build can be for. It is not what the site is for. LEGO® stays out of
  headings, so a heading says "our local robotics club" and the running
  text says "LEGO® robotics club".
- The footer disclaimer sentence is verbatim from the LEGO Group's Fair Play page and
  is not to be paraphrased. The Elenco® line under it is ours, written to
  match; Elenco has no fan-site policy to quote.
- Elenco® and Snap Circuits® take ® on every mention, the same as LEGO®.
