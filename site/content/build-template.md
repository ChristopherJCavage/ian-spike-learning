# Build page -- section order

Use this order for every build page. Copy lives in `builds/<slug>.md`;
facts come from the build's `README.md`, `parts.txt`, `model.ldr`, and
`preflight.md` in the repo.

1. **Breadcrumb and Goes With.** Every build starts `Projects >`. A project
   of several continues `{Project} > {Build}`, plus a chip linking each
   sibling. A project of one is `Projects > {Build}` and has no Goes With
   chip.
2. **Hero.** Name as H1, the kit badge, a Club-Ready or Home Build tag,
   and a one- or two-sentence lead that says what a kid does with it, not
   how it works. The photo (Photo frame / Hero) is the picture; the LeoCAD
   render sits beside it as a small "As Drawn" thumbnail.
3. **Facts row.** `{parts} parts · {steps} steps · {program type}`, then
   either "about one session" (Club-Ready) or who builds the hard part
   (a Home Build).
4. **Get It.** Print the PDF (print notes underneath) and Click Through
   the Steps.
5. **How It Works.** The mechanism in plain words, then the program
   picture and two or three sentences on the code. The picture is a
   screenshot of the program from the SPIKE™ App on the iPad, cropped to
   the blocks, in slot `photos/<slug>/program` (Screenshot treatment).
   Scenery builds use "What's on It" instead and have no program.
6. **What You Need.** The kit indicator for the build's family. A SPIKE™
   build also lists the hub (charged) and the SPIKE™ App. Anything to make
   ahead of time goes here too.
7. **Small print.** "Original model, not one of the official {family}
   lessons." A SPIKE™ build reads "official SPIKE™ lessons."

Rules: no caps eyebrows containing a trademark; "color" not "colour";
"model of a drone", never "flying drone".
