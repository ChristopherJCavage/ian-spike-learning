## Half-pipe Swing

Leo, one of the four minifigures from the Essential box, rides a
skateboard on the end of a swinging arm inside a curved half-pipe. Press
the button and he drops in, swings up to one lip and back to the other
three times, then settles in the middle. A grown-up builds the frame. Ian
builds the rider and the ramp.

## How It Works

The ramp is four quarter-circle gear racks from the Expansion box, two
rails with the teeth facing in. Two gears under the skateboard roll along
those teeth, one in each rail, while the Large Motor swings the arm. Leo
rides in the gap between the rails.

Press the force sensor and he drops in. An arrow on the hub's 5x5 shows
which way he is going, and at each lip the 3x3 light matrix on the front
flashes orange and the hub beeps. After three passes each way he stops in
the middle, the front lights go green, the hub smiles, and it plays a
sound. The program is Word Blocks, because a swing needs an angle and Icon
Blocks only turn whole rotations. A grown-up reads the blocks. Ian taps
them in.

## What You Need

- All three boxes on the table. Essential for Leo, the skateboard, the
  baseplates, the long green bricks, and the 3x3 light matrix. Prime for
  the Large Hub, the Large Motor, the force sensor, the beams, the gears,
  and the pins. Expansion for the four curved racks.
- The Large Hub, charged. Motor in port A, force sensor in port B, light
  matrix in port C.
- A tablet or laptop with the official SPIKE™ App.
- A grown-up for the frame: the posts, the motor, the hub, and the three
  axles through the ramp. Ian builds Leo, the board, and the ramp, and
  presses the button.
