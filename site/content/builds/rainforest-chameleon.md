## Chameleon Color-Match

A chameleon that looks at a color and changes its back to match. Show it
red and it flashes a warning instead of hiding.

## How It Works

Its head, looking down, is the color sensor, and its back is the 3x3 light
matrix. Between them they use both of the hub's ports, so there's no
motor. Hold a colored pad under its head and its back lights the same
color. That's a fair first explanation of camouflage. Hold a red pad under
it and the back goes red anyway, because some animals shout "don't eat me"
instead of hiding. It has ball-joint legs, round white eyes, and a tail
that turns a corner.

Picture: screenshot of the program from the SPIKE™ App on the iPad,
cropped to the blocks. Slot: `photos/rainforest-chameleon/program`.

The program is four short stacks, one for each pad color, because Icon
Blocks don't have an if-else. Each stack waits for its color and sets the
matrix to match. Nothing turns the light off when the pad slides away, so
the last color stays lit. That's how Icon Blocks work, not a mistake.

## What You Need

- One SPIKE™ Essential set: the Small Hub (charged), the color sensor, and
  the light matrix.
- A tablet or laptop with the official SPIKE™ App.
- Five color pads, made ahead from the same box: one 2x4 tile each
  in blue, red, yellow, dark green, and azure, each on a plate so it
  slides.
