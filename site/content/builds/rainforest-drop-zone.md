## Rainforest Drop Zone

The patch of forest the Seed Drone flies over, with a red band across the
middle where a tree used to be. It's the short build, with no electronics,
and it's done in about ten minutes.

Facts: 17 parts · 9 steps · no program · about ten minutes

## Get It

- **Print the PDF** -- 10 pages · US Letter · print in color · 9 MB
- **Click Through the Steps**

## What's on It

A long white plate with a green wall down each side, a tree at each end,
some grass, a frog, and a ranger holding the drone's controller. Two red
tiles cross the lane between the walls, and that red band is what the
drone's color sensor is looking for. Each step adds one kind of piece, so
it's a good first build for the youngest kids in the room.

## What You Need

- The plates, tiles, trees, frog, and figure from one SPIKE™ Essential set,
  and nothing from the electronics. It shares no parts with the Seed
  Drone, so both can be built from the same box at once.
- The Seed Drone, to fly over it. On its own it's just a nice diorama.

Small print: Original model, not one of the official SPIKE™ lesson units.
