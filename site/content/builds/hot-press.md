## How It Works

The motor sits high on a red tower, pinned by two pegs through its back.
One peg in the motor's own hub is the crank. It pulls a blue beam, and
the beam pushes a green plate down two black axles. The red brick under
the plate is the press head. It stops just over the wood, so the motor
never jams on it.

Picture: screenshot of the program from the SPIKE™ App on the iPad,
cropped to the blocks. Slot: `photos/hot-press/program`.

The program is one stack. The 3x3 matrix turns red, the motor turns
half a turn and the press comes down, the hub plays a sound, the motor
turns half a turn more and the press goes back up, and the matrix turns
green. The wood doesn't get thinner. The single thin plate in front of
the press is the picture of wood after a real press. The red bricks, a
red crystal, and the red matrix stand in for heat. Nothing is heated.

- A tablet or laptop with the official SPIKE™ App. Essential runs the
