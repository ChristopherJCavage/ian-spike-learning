## Seed Drone

A handheld drone that hunts for the red band in the forest and drops a
seed on it.

## How It Works

A color sensor points down from under the body. When it sees red, a small
motor tips the little tray on the tail and a loose red ball, the seed,
rolls out. The big two-blade rotor on top spins when you flick it but isn't
powered. The Small Hub only has two ports, and the sensor and the tray
motor use both.

Picture: screenshot of the program from the SPIKE™ App on the iPad,
cropped to the blocks. Slot: `photos/seed-drone/program`.

The program is one stack. When the sensor on port A sees red, the motor on
port B turns a quarter turn to tip the tray, the hub plays a sound while
the seed rolls out, and the motor turns back so the tray is level for the
next one.

Editor note (not on the page): the screenshot in this slot shows a
different wiring. The sensor is on B, the motor is on A, and the motor
turns half a turn each way and then stops, with no sound. The paragraph
above stays, because it matches `builds/seed-drone/program.spike`.

## What You Need

- One SPIKE™ Essential set: the Small Hub (charged), one small motor, and
  the color sensor.
- A tablet or laptop with the official SPIKE™ App. Essential runs the
  program from the tablet, so keep it connected while kids play.
- The Rainforest Drop Zone, or any red patch about four studs wide on a
  white surface.
- One of the red balls from the box as the seed. There are five.

Small print: Original model, not one of the official SPIKE™ lesson units.
