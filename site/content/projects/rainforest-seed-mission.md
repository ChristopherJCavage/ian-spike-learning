## Rainforest Seed Mission

Someone cut down a tree in the rainforest. Your drone has to find the bare
patch and plant a new seed. It's two builds that work together: the survey
drone, and the strip of forest it flies over.

Facts: 2 builds · 75 parts · one SPIKE™ Essential box · about one session

## How They Work Together

Both builds come out of one SPIKE™ Essential box, and you can make them in
the same sitting. But the real point is that they work together. In the
club, the lesson was how drones can help a rainforest grow back by dropping
seeds into the bare patches where trees were cut down. The Drop Zone is a
strip of forest with a red band where a tree used to be. The Seed Drone
flies over it, and when its color sensor sees the red band, a motor tips its
tray and the seed drops.

## Talking About It

Say up front that it's a model and won't take off. Real drones also map
damaged forest from the air, so planting crews know where to go. A good
question to ask afterward is why the rotor doesn't spin on its own. The
hub only has two ports, so an engineer has to choose which parts get a
motor and which just come along for the ride.
