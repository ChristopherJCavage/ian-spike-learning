## Why We Started Making Our Own

The paragraphs are the full content width, the same right edge as the two
doors below. A narrow column here stops short of the pictures.

We've already worked through every LEGO® SPIKE™ Essential unit in the LEGO® SPIKE™ App.
Ian's still a little young to explore the app on his own without a lesson to
follow, so I make new lessons for him with [LeoCAD](https://www.leocad.org/) (free, open-source
building software), Anthropic's Claude models, xAI's Grok models, and a
few custom Python scripts.

We're also part of the LEGO® robotics club at the Contemporary Chinese
School of Arizona (CCSA), and when the coach needs another lesson, I help
out there too. This year's theme was rainforests, and the app didn't have
enough lessons to fill the semester. That's where the Seed Drone, the Drop
Zone, the Chameleon, and the Hot Press came from.

The Elenco® Snap Circuits® manuals are the official experiments, and they
didn't quite land for us. A page tells you
to snap a part, and not what to look for. I wanted each page to do one
job: a problem, a guess, a test, and then the reason.

That's The Spark Detectives. Sully, Tika, Pip, and Coco run a little
detective agency, and each case is one circuit, told as a comic. I took
the shape of those pages from Beast Academy®, the math comic from Art of
Problem Solving®. Ian loves those books. The way a lesson moves is
theirs. The characters and the cases are ours.

I made this site for other parents with young kids. Maybe you have a LEGO® SPIKE™
set or two, maybe an Elenco® Snap Circuits® kit, or maybe you're just looking for
ideas to keep STEM going at home. Use any of it however you like.

The LEGO Group stopped selling SPIKE™ on June 30, 2026, but the SPIKE™ App
is supported through June 30, 2031. The Simple Machines set was retired
years earlier, but it's all gears, levers, pulleys, and wheels, with no app
or batteries, so it doesn't go out of date. You can still find both
secondhand or from resellers, and they're still a great way to teach kids
how machines work. [Kits](/kits)
